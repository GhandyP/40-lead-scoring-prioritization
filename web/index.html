Scores range from 0 to 100 points. Missing optional timeline or interaction inputs are labeled missing, not presented as known zero values.

Illustrative, not predictive. All people and companies here are fictional. The score is an advisory rule-based aid, not a calibrated conversion probability or a substitute for a person's judgment.
